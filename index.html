    How it works

    1. 01

      One origin, no keys

      The page only talks to its own server. Upstream URLs are fixed server-side; the browser sends stop ids, which are checked against the official stop list before anything leaves the box.

    2. 02

      Shared cache

      Each source is hit at most once per window — departures 20 s, weather 10 min, prices 15 min — however many screens are open. Simultaneous misses share one request.

    3. 03

      Degrades, never lies

      6-second timeouts and back-off. When a source fails the last good answer is served and marked stale with its age, instead of a blank panel or invented numbers.

    4. 04

      Riga time, exactly

      Clocks run in Europe/Riga whatever your device says. Departures arrive as service-day seconds (a 03:00 night bus is "27:00") and are folded against Riga time, corrected for your clock's drift.
